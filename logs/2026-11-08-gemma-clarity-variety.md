## Conclusion and next direction

Do not roll out an application-wide prompt/model switch from this evidence.
Gemma can preserve conditions, named manual actions, negative retry constraints
and author/publication constraints on these two short successful examples, but
preservation is not consistent across this small set and a tiny invitation input
timed out. Neither short prompt nor model choice establishes guaranteed fidelity.

inline review and explicit save/publication, assess actual usefulness, and retain
